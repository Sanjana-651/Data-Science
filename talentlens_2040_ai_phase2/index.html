Foundation · secure MVP

Authentication, role permissions, encrypted file intake, consent, retention and deletion policies.

P2
Document intelligence

PDF/DOCX extraction, OCR for scans, format validation, and reviewer-correctable text previews.

Structured job criteria, semantic retrieval, evidence citations, calibrated confidence, and auditable scoring.

P4
Trust, evaluation & deployment

Bias and accessibility tests, security review, monitoring, human decision gates, and staged pilot.

Prototype integrity notice. The future-facing visuals are a design concept, not a claim about technology available in 2040. All listed candidate profiles and scores are fictional. The local backend extracts resume text and calculates transparent keyword-based matching scores; it does not call an external AI model or make hiring decisions. Do not use this prototype to make employment decisions.
